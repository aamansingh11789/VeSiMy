# VeSiMy First-Week Email Sequence

Paste these into Sender.net (or whatever email tool you use). Schedule them as a 5-email automation triggered when a new user signs up. Tone is direct, helpful, founder-from-the-floor. No emojis. No fluff. No fake urgency.

## Email 3 — Send 3 days after signup
**Subject:** Most users find their bottleneck in step 3

Hi {{first_name}},

Quick observation from talking to other VeSiMy users: most people find a bottleneck they did not know existed by the third process step they map.

The pattern is usually the same. You map the obvious steps (the ones you think about). Then you map a transition step (handoff between people, approval wait, batch). That transition step has 4x the wait time of anything else. That is your bottleneck.

If you have your map open, try this: look at every step where the work changes hands. Add the actual wait time. Most people are surprised.

**[Open your project →](https://vesimy.com/dashboard)**

Max

## Setup Instructions

1. Log into Sender.net (or your tool of choice)
2. Create a new automation
3. Trigger: "User added to list" (your signup list)
4. Add 5 email steps with the delays: 0 days, 1 day, 3 days, 5 days, 7 days
5. Paste each email above into the corresponding step
6. Make sure {{first_name}} is the correct merge tag in your tool
7. Send a test to yourself before going live
8. Activate

The whole setup takes about 30 minutes. Do it once and it runs forever.

## What to track

After 30 days, look at:
- Open rate per email (email 1 should be 50%+, others 25%+)
- Reply rate (any reply is a win at this stage)
- Click-through to dashboard
- Did the user complete the activation checklist?

If email 5 generates 1 in 10 replies, you have a working sequence and a steady stream of user feedback.
