## PRIVACY POLICY ADDITIONS
### Add to existing Privacy Policy as new section: "What We Collect and Why"

### Tier 0 Session Data (New in v4.0)

When you use the free process mapping tool at vesimy.com/start without creating an account, we collect the following:

- Your email address (used to send your report and, if you opt in, our newsletter)
- Your first name if provided (used to personalize your report)
- The industry you select
- The process name you enter
- The step names and time estimates you add
- Your pain point selection
- Your improvement target category

This data is used only to generate your lean report and to send you the report by email. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with any third party other than our email delivery provider (Sender) and AI analysis provider (Anthropic) as described below.

Tier 0 session data is retained for 30 days. If you have not created a VeSiMy account within 30 days, your process data is permanently deleted. Your email address remains in our system only if you have not unsubscribed from our email list. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email we send.

If you create a VeSiMy account within 30 days of a Tier 0 session, we offer to import your session data into your new account. This import is optional and requires your explicit confirmation. If you decline, the session data is deleted at the 30-day mark regardless.

---

### Team Collaboration Data (New in v4.0)

When you use VeSiMy as part of an enterprise team, additional data is collected related to collaborative use:

- Which team members accessed a shared project and when
- Which steps or elements of a map were edited by which team member
- Comments and annotations added to the map, including the author and timestamp
- Changes made during observation sessions, including the data entered and the session duration
- Authority level assignments and any changes to them

This data is used to support real-time collaboration, to maintain an audit trail for data integrity purposes, and to provide the team leader with visibility into project activity. It is not used for any purpose outside of the VeSiMy service.

The team leader is responsible for ensuring that team members are informed that their activity on shared projects is visible to the account holder and to authorized administrators.

---

### Mobile Offline Data (New in v4.0)

When you use VeSiMy in offline mode on a mobile device during a floor observation session, data you enter is stored locally on your device until a network connection is available. This locally stored data includes:

- Cycle time measurements recorded with the stopwatch
- Changeover time entries
- WIP counts
- Defect rate entries
- Any step-level data you enter during observation

This data is transmitted to our servers when your device reconnects to the internet. If you uninstall the VeSiMy app before the data syncs, locally stored data may be lost. We recommend completing a sync before uninstalling or making any changes to your device.

Locally stored offline data is encrypted on the device using AES-256. We do not have access to locally stored data before it is transmitted to our servers.

---

### Skill Matrix Data (New in v4.0)

VeSiMy collects behavioral data related to how you use the platform in order to generate skill matrix assessments and AI coaching notes. This includes:

- Time spent on each phase of a VSM project
- Number of steps in each project
- Frequency of stopwatch use versus manual time entry
- Lap count consistency in cycle time measurement
- Completeness of data fields per project
- Which methodology tools you use and how frequently
- Which AI recommendations you act on

This data is used only to generate your skill matrix profile and AI coaching notes within VeSiMy. It is not shared with your employer, with any third party, or with any other user. Enterprise team leaders can see team-level aggregate skill matrix data but cannot see individual team members' behavioral data in granular form.

You can request deletion of your skill matrix data at any time. This will reset your maturity level and coaching notes. Historical projects will not be affected.

---

### AI Report Data (New in v4.0)

When you generate an AI lean report in VeSiMy, your VSM data (step names, cycle times, WIP counts, defect rates, activity descriptions, and related process data) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the report.

This data is used only for the purpose of generating your report. It is not used to train Anthropic's AI models. VeSiMy has a data processing agreement with Anthropic that prohibits use of customer data for model training.

Your VSM data is not visible to VeSiMy employees in the normal course of business. Access to VSM data is restricted to authorized personnel for support, security, and legal compliance purposes only.

---

### Version History Data (New in v4.0)

VeSiMy stores all versions of your VSM maps permanently as long as your account is active. This includes every saved state of every project across all versions.

If you delete your account, all version history data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

If you delete a specific project, all versions of that project are permanently deleted within 30 days.

Version data is not recoverable after deletion. If you are concerned about losing version history, export a copy of your maps before deleting a project or account.

### Enterprise Team Leader Responsibilities

If you use VeSiMy as an enterprise team leader with the authority to invite team members and assign access levels, you agree to:

1. Only invite individuals who have consented to have their activity on shared projects visible to the team account holder.

2. Ensure team members understand what data is collected about their platform activity and how it is used, as described in our Privacy Policy.

3. Take responsibility for the accuracy and appropriateness of data entered into shared projects by team members under your account.

4. Comply with applicable data protection regulations in your jurisdiction regarding the collection and retention of team member activity data through VeSiMy.

5. Not use VeSiMy's collaboration features in ways that would violate the privacy rights of your team members under applicable law.

VeSiMy is not responsible for the use of team collaboration features in ways that violate these terms. Enterprise accounts that are found to be in violation may have collaboration features suspended pending review.

---

### Version Toggle and Migration Window

When VeSiMy releases a new version of the platform, existing users will be given a minimum 90-day window to migrate to the new version.

During this window:

- Your existing data and projects remain fully accessible.
- You can choose to migrate to the new version at any time within the window.
- If you migrate, your data is preserved and accessible in the new version.
- If you do not migrate before the window closes, your data will be migrated automatically. You will receive email notifications at 60 days, 30 days, and 7 days before the migration date.

VeSiMy will not migrate your data in ways that result in data loss. If any data cannot be migrated, you will be notified in advance and provided with an export option for affected data.

### Lifetime Plan Terms

The VeSiMy Lifetime Plan, where available, grants access to the Pro feature set as it exists at the time of purchase and to all future Pro-tier feature releases for the lifetime of the VeSiMy platform.

"Pro-tier feature releases" means features added to the Pro plan after your purchase. It does not include Enterprise-tier features unless those features are subsequently moved to the Pro plan.

The Lifetime Plan is for individual use by the account holder. It is non-transferable.

In the event that VeSiMy discontinues the platform, Lifetime Plan holders will receive a minimum 12-month notice period and a full refund of the purchase price less any applicable fees.

The 33% enterprise discount benefit for Lifetime Plan holders applies to a single enterprise contract entered into within 24 months of the Lifetime Plan purchase. It cannot be applied to renewals or additional contracts.

### AI Report Disclaimer

AI-generated lean reports in VeSiMy provide analysis, recommendations, and projections based on the VSM data you enter and the lean methodology knowledge base embedded in the platform.

These reports are analytical tools, not guarantees. Specifically:

- Lead time improvement projections are estimates based on lean methodology standards and industry benchmarks. Actual results depend on implementation quality, workforce capability, organizational factors, and variables outside the scope of the analysis.

- Waste identification and classification is based on the data and activity descriptions you provide. Incomplete or inaccurate data will produce less accurate analysis.

- Tool recommendations reflect best-practice lean methodology. They do not account for regulatory requirements specific to your industry or jurisdiction. You are responsible for ensuring that improvement actions comply with applicable regulations.

- AI coaching notes reflect patterns in platform usage data. They are intended as development prompts, not performance evaluations.

VeSiMy's AI reports are designed by lean practitioners with real operational experience. They reflect sound methodology. They are not a substitute for qualified lean expertise in high-stakes operational decisions.
