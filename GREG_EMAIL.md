## What's new in v4.0 (for your awareness)

Based on your specific feedback, here is what was addressed:

**Bug fix you reported:** Project deletion now works correctly on both logins. The delete button previously redirected to dashboard without actually calling the delete API. That is fixed.

**Simulation tool (your request):** The stress scenario simulation now shows more detailed descriptions of what happens at each pressure state — queue dynamics, labor shortages, equipment failure scenarios. Changeable parameters are on the roadmap for the next release.

**New tools added:**
- OODA Loop — for fast operational decisions
- 8D Report — for quality escapes with customer impact
- DMAIC — for data-driven complex problems

**New entry point:** vesimy.com/start — anyone can map a process and get an AI lean report without creating an account. Great for sharing with colleagues who haven't tried VeSiMy yet.

**VeSiMy Guided:** A new 8-step guided flow that teaches lean methodology while the user maps their first process. Built for teams where not everyone has a lean background.
